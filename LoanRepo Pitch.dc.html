01 · The mechanism

When rates rise, banks extend your tenure instead of raising your EMI.

Extending the term needs no consent and no conversation. Raising the EMI needs both. So the default is the silent one, and the borrower's only monitoring instrument — the amount leaving their account each month — is the one thing engineered not to change.

02 · The problem

Borrowers can't see what their own loan is doing to them.

A bank statement shows this month's balance. It never shows the counterfactual — what your tenure would have been if the rate hadn't moved, or what you should ask for. That gap sits there for the entire life of the loan.

05 · Why this wins trust

"Anytime is a good time to buy" survives because everyone positioned to correct it profits from you believing it.

Banks & comparison sites
Earn on disbursal. The incentive is to say yes, and to say now.
Builders & agents
Paid on the transaction, not on the outcome that follows it.
LoanRepo
Earns the same whether you buy, wait, prepay or refinance.
No competitor calculator — RupayWise, ET Money, Groww, MoneyKarma — runs against real repo and MCLR history, and most earn lender referral commissions. The moat is the conflict of interest, not the code.

08 · The retention problem, and the answer

A shock is not a habit.

Someone runs their loan, sees +7.4 years, feels the floor move — and has no reason to return. That is the honest weakness of a calculator, and pretending otherwise would be the wrong way to build this.

The answer was already in the product: every floating loan has a reset date, and it is the borrower's alone. That date is a calendar we did not have to invent.

09 · Business model

Revenue that never depends on which choice you make.

Tracked loans
One loan free forever. ₹149/month for as many as you have — a spouse's, a parent's, a second property. Live today.
MPC-day alerts
The RBI meets about six times a year. Subscribers get "what just changed for your loan" — the recurring hook.
B2B licensing
License the rate-history engine to fee-only RIAs, HR wellness platforms and finance creators.
Corporate workshops
Loan-literacy sessions sold to employers, drawing directly on ten years of client-facing advisory work.
Explicitly avoided: lender lead-generation, builder advertising, and loan-comparison affiliate links — the exact model most competitors run on, and the most lucrative one available to us.
